Interactive career workshops

Four courses. Choose one.

Students select the course that best matches their interests. Each course is delivered by a specialized provider as three interactive sessions over two days, 4.5 hours in total.

Each student may choose only ONE of the four courses
Course 1 3 sessions · 4.5 hours

Ready for Work

Empowering the Next Generation of Leaders

Catalysts 4 Change (C4C) goes beyond traditional classrooms with immersive programs in diplomacy, entrepreneurship, sustainability, camps, and global conferences. At this conference, C4C runs a dynamic, hands-on workshop that equips high school students with the skills to navigate university life and the professional world.

The three sessions
  • Communication & Community. Unspoken workplace rules, assumptions, and an introduction to the SEL (Social and Emotional Learning) framework using the CASEL competencies.
  • Leadership. Trust, communication with managers, managing responsibility, and how leaders carry context that teams may not initially see.
  • Followership. Teamwork, offering honest input, knowing when to question, and how effective followership shapes group results.
What students take away
  • A shared vocabulary to reflect on their communication and collaboration styles.
  • A deeper understanding of when to step up as a leader, when to follow, and when to ask questions.
  • Practical career preparation, including interview tips, guidance on building a references bank, and a personal action plan for ongoing development.
Delivered by Catalysts 4 Change (C4C)
Course 2 3 sessions · 4.5 hours

Steer Your Career

Explore. Develop. Prepare for Your Future.

Presented by INJAZ Egypt, a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering young people to achieve economic success through entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and career readiness. Steer Your Career is an interactive career exploration experience where students build workplace skills, discover career pathways, and learn how to prepare for internships and jobs.

The programme covers
  • Building essential skills. Leadership and time management.
  • Preparing for the world of work. Internship and job opportunities, CV writing and interview skills.
What students gain
  • Practical career readiness and employability skills.
  • Greater awareness of different career pathways and opportunities.
  • Confidence in presenting themselves professionally.
  • Valuable insights into the workplace through interaction with industry professionals.
  • A stronger understanding of how to prepare for future internships and employment.
Delivered by INJAZ Egypt
Course 3 3 sessions · 4.5 hours

Career Success

Strong soft skills. Clear priorities. A professional brand.

Developed by INJAZ Egypt, a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering young people to achieve economic success through entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and career readiness. Career Success is an interactive career exploration experience built around four objectives, each with a clear goal for students.

The programme covers
  • Strong Soft Skills. Identify in-demand soft skills and apply them in interview settings.
  • Know Your Work Priorities. Clarify work environment priorities and maintain focus, proactivity, and adaptability during career exploration.
  • Know Who Is Hiring. Research high-growth industries (like STEM) and identify the education and training needed to remain competitive.
  • Know Your Personal Brand. Master job-search tools and build a positive, professional personal brand for career advancement.
Course provider: INJAZ Egypt. Delivered by Ms. Rasha Saad (Beyond School Walls and Academic Director) and Ms. Maram Arafat (Beyond School Walls and Student Leadership Coordinator).
Course 4 3 sessions · 4.5 hours

Own Your Voice

Public Speaking for Young Communicators

Founded in 1999, Quest was born from a vision to revolutionize corporate employee development through innovative, high-impact learning. At this conference, Quest offers an interactive public speaking and presentation skills programme that helps students communicate confidently, express their ideas effectively, and engage an audience, whether presenting in class, taking part in university activities, or preparing for a career.

The three sessions
  • Find Your Voice. Overcoming speaking nerves, building confidence, and strategies to manage public speaking anxiety.
  • Bring Your Message to Life. Strengthening voice projection, body language, eye contact, and presentation skills to deliver messages with impact.
  • Connect With Your Audience. Creating engaging presentations, encouraging audience interaction, and responding to questions with confidence.
What students gain
  • Greater confidence in public speaking and presenting.
  • Stronger verbal and non-verbal communication skills.
  • The ability to structure ideas and deliver clear, impactful messages.
  • Techniques to capture audience attention and encourage engagement.
  • Confidence in handling questions and thinking on their feet.
  • Practical experience through speaking activities, mini-presentations, and constructive feedback.
Delivered by QUEST
